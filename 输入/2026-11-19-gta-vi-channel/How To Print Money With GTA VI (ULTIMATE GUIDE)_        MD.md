## I. Creating the account

the first step of this challenge is making the account

but not every YouTube account gets a fair shot at going viral

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HXWf3imW4AARMvr?format=jpg&name=large)

you can start a brand new channel if you want, but there are a few tricks that help your channel break the algorithm

trick 1: get an aged account

the first one is getting an aged account

the logic behind buying one: you're getting an account that was created long before botting became a thing

it has years of normal history behind it, so it doesn't look like 1 of the thousands of fresh accounts bot farms spin up every day

> **one warning.** buying accounts goes against YouTube's terms, and you never know an account's full history. only buy from a seller who hands over full ownership, change the email, password and recovery details the same day, and never link it to your main channel

step 1: lock the account down

- **bought an aged account?** change the email, password and recovery phone the day you get it, then turn on 2-step verification
- **starting fresh?** make a new Gmail just for the channel and turn on 2-step verification on day 1

you'll need 2-step for the Partner Program anyway, and GTA channels get hijacked fast

step 2: create the channel under a name

in YouTube, create the channel with a custom name instead of using your personal profile

that makes it a brand account, so later you can add an editor or thumbnail designer as a manager w/o handing over your password

step 3: name and handle

pick something short and easy to say that's clearly about GTA VI. your handle should match the name exactly

2 rules:

1. **don't make it look official.** keep Rockstar's logo and name out of it so it never reads like the game's own channel
2. **leave room to grow.** "GTA 6 Money Guides" dies when the money guides do, but a name tied to you or to Leonida lasts the whole life of the game

step 4: verify your phone

**YouTube Studio > settings > channel > feature eligibility > verify phone number**

this turns on 3 things:

- videos longer than 15 minutes
- custom thumbnails
- live streaming

skip it and you can't even upload your own thumbnail, which kills a long-form channel before it starts

advanced features like pinned comments need phone verification plus channel history or an ID check. they usually show up after a few weeks of clean uploads

step 5: set your country

**settings > channel > basic info > country**

set the country you live in. payments, tax forms and Partner Program checks all match against it

step 6: branding

- **profile picture:** 1 bold image you can read at tiny size, like a character silhouette or a single letter in Vice City pink
- **banner:** channel name, what you post and your schedule ("new GTA VI guides every day")
- **description:** the first 2 lines say exactly what the channel is, using words people search like GTA 6 money, GTA 6 secrets and GTA 6 guides. add your X link

step 7: upload defaults

**settings > upload defaults**, set these once:

- **category:** gaming, with the game title set to Grand Theft Auto VI
- **language:** English
- **visibility:** private, so nothing goes live before you've checked the thumbnail and the first 7 seconds

checklist before your first upload

- ☐ aged account secured or fresh Gmail made, 2-step verification on
- ☐ channel created with a custom name
- ☐ handle matches the name
- ☐ phone verified, custom thumbnails turned on
- ☐ country set to where you live
- ☐ profile picture, banner and description done
- ☐ upload defaults set

## III. Editing and making videos

i'm not going to recommend AI video tools or any of the easy one-click editors for this

the reason: to make an AI video that looks good, you need to be extremely good at using AI. if you're not, you're just making AI slop

and YouTube is already cutting that off. since July 2025, mass-produced and repetitive videos count as inauthentic content, and channels full of them lose monetization

so we're editing the videos ourselves, to get exactly the result we want

what to edit with

CapCut works. so does any other editing software you already have, basic or advanced

don't have anything yet? start with whatever you can get your hands on. **a good edit in CapCut beats a lazy edit in Premiere every time**

the standard

we're making high quality long-form videos, because long-form is what brings the money

low effort videos have no place in this plan, so be careful here

turn the radio off

GTA's radio stations are full of licensed songs, and they trigger Content ID claims. when that happens, the song's owner takes the ad money from your video

turn the in-game radio off while you record, and use music you have the rights to in the edit

the first 7 seconds rule

in September, YouTube changed its gaming ad rules right before GTA VI

- graphic violence in the **first 7 seconds** of a video earns no ad money. the exact same clip from second 8 onward can carry full ads
- shock montages and mass NPC killing sprees earn nothing, wherever they sit in the video
- in-game drug content moved into the eligible column, which helps a lot in a game set in a version of Florida

so **open on the payoff**: the money counter, the hidden spot, the finished garage. save the shootout for second 8
