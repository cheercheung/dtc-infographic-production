## How To Actually Use These Hooks

Don't copy them word for word. That gets you flagged and it's lazy. Here's the system:

1. Pick the 4-5 families that match your customer's awareness level. Problem-unaware buyers need Did You Know and The Real Reason. Solution-aware buyers need I Tried Every and Before You Buy. Hot audiences get Obsessed and the offer-style hooks.
2. Rewrite 10 hooks per family with YOUR product's specifics: your mechanism, your sufferer, your numbers. The skeleton stays, the meat changes.
3. Test them as statics FIRST, at 50-100 variations a day. Vary the CONCEPT, not just the opening line: fifty hooks on one piece of footage is still one Entity ID.
4. Pair every copy hook with a VISUAL hook from Part 2 before it goes to an editor.

**13 families × 18 visual types = 234 combinations. You'll run out of budget before you run out of concepts.**

Here's the prompt that does step 2 for you. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT:

Write 50 hooks: 10 per family, each under 12 words, each with my product's specific mechanism, sufferer or number filled in. No generic versions. Then rank the top 10 by how specific and believable they are, and tell me why in one line each.
