# 使用提示词

将用户给出的 Get your product ads inside GTA 做成一张英文信息图，展示街头广告牌、便利店冷柜和游戏房三类 GTA 风格产品植入样片，继续说明三镜头结构、包装一致性和最后的广告落版。产品品类参考上一份原稿的 Ads for Brands & UGC Content 一节，镜头设计作为本次创意示例

采用深色六卡单图模板。画布以 800 × 1000 CSS px 排版，使用 2.7 倍导出 2160 × 2700 px、4:5 PNG。背景炭黑，左右边距 36 CSS px。顶部使用细深灰窗口栏，放红、黄、绿三个小圆点与 `GTA / Product placement` 导航

主标题左对齐加珊瑚橙星芒。第一行用大号白色 Georgia 粗体排 `Get your product`，第二行用同号薄荷绿 Georgia 粗斜体排 `ads inside GTA`。下面用浅灰 Arial 排 `Three ways to place a product in a GTA-inspired spec ad.`

正文按两列三行布局。第一组 `Place the product` 放在顶行，范围 `01–02`。第二组 `Build the ad` 位于后两行，范围 `03–06`。每张卡使用比背景稍亮的深灰、细描边、小圆角、薄荷绿两位编号与白色粗体标题。每行左侧用小线框方形和浅色 Arial 正文，右侧用薄荷绿、浅黄、灰、珊瑚橙或粉色标签，行间使用低对比细线

底部留暗色间距，放 64 CSS px 高的满宽薄荷绿栏，居中排黑色粗体 `Comment GTA to get the playbook`，`GTA` 用黑色 Georgia 粗斜体

- keywords：GTA
- 最终 CTA：Comment GTA to get the playbook

## 图内正文

### Place the product

#### 01 Street billboard

- Put the product on a city billboard — PLACEMENT
- Reveal it as the camera drives by — DRIVE-BY
- Cut closer so the brand is readable — CLOSE-UP

#### 02 Corner-store fridge

- Stock a branded can on the shelf — PLACEMENT
- Open the fridge for the reveal — REVEAL
- Lift the can with the label facing camera — PRODUCT

### Build the ad

#### 03 Gaming-room setup

- Place a chair or controller in the room — PLACEMENT
- Show a player using it — ACTION
- Cut to the detail you want to sell — FEATURE

#### 04 Cut a three-shot sequence

- Open on the location — WIDE
- Reveal the product in the scene — REVEAL
- Cut to a clean view of the product — PACK SHOT

#### 05 Keep the brand readable

- Match the real packaging and label — ACCURACY
- Keep the design consistent across shots — MATCH
- Hold a steady shot of the logo — LEGIBILITY

#### 06 Finish with an offer

- Give viewers one reason to buy — BENEFIT
- Put the offer on the end card — OFFER
- Give viewers one clear next step — CTA

## 首稿校验

逐张核对卡片内容与分组，检查 390 px 宽下的正文、标题与底栏。保留原题用词，核对 GTA 拼写、产品标签和领取 CTA
