# 使用提示词

把用户提供的 Markdown 的第 2 节 Ads for Brands & UGC Content 提炼成一张单页英文信息图，重点是怎样把品牌广告和 GTA VI 受众结合，并用样片开发品牌合作。保留原文 10 个品牌、每个品牌 5–10 条广告、Higgsfield、X 展示流程和 Grok 找品牌团队。把原文提到的游戏椅、能量饮料与游戏配件各转成一个示意广告 hook，在成图中统一标为 Example GTA VI hooks。最后一张卡片使用透明的来源描述呈现作者自述的 Instagram UGC 数据

按深色六卡版式制作 2160 × 2700 px、4:5 英文 PNG。以 800 × 1000 CSS px 排版并按 2.7 倍导出。背景炭黑，左右边距 36 CSS px。顶部用细窄深灰窗口栏，包含红、黄、绿三个圆点及 `GTA VI / UGC ads` 导航

标题区左对齐，加珊瑚橙星芒。第一行用白色 Georgia 粗体排 `Make UGC ads`，第二行用薄荷绿 Georgia 粗斜体排 `around GTA VI`。下方用浅灰无衬线字排 `Find brands targeting GTA VI players, make sample ads, then pitch the team.`

正文两列三行，首行归至 Find the fit，后两行归至 Make the ads and pitch，分别标上 01–02 和 03–06。卡片使用偏亮深灰底、细描边和小圆角，每张卡片以薄荷绿两位数编号加白色粗标题起头。条目左侧使用小线框标记与浅色 Arial 正文，右侧排固定宽度的薄荷绿、奶油黄、灰色、珊瑚橙或粉色标签。行与行之间用细灰线分隔。最后一张卡片右侧用更大的数字标签呈现结果

卡片下方小字标注 `Source: Miles Deutscher, “Ads for Brands & UGC Content”`。底部留暗色间距，再加 64 CSS px 高的满宽薄荷绿横幅，居中排黑色粗体 `Comment GTA to get the playbook`，其中 `GTA` 用黑色 Georgia 粗斜体

- 最终 CTA：Comment GTA to get the playbook

## 图内正文

### Find the fit

#### 01 Find GTA VI advertisers

- Search the Meta Ad Library — SEARCH
- Look for gaming ads tied to GTA VI — FILTER
- Shortlist brands that fit — 10 BRANDS

#### 02 Example GTA VI hooks

- “My GTA VI setup upgrade” — CHAIR
- “My fuel for GTA VI game night” — DRINK
- “Trying my new controller in GTA VI” — GEAR

### Make the ads and pitch

#### 03 Make sample ads

- Write variants per brand — 5–10 ADS
- Use Higgsfield to make the creative — AI TOOL
- Give the product a role in the GTA VI hook — PRODUCT

#### 04 Show your work on X

- Show who made the ad — INTRO
- Walk through the creative process — PROCESS
- Share the finished work and results — RESULTS

#### 05 Pitch the brand team

- Find team members with Grok — FIND
- Reach out to them directly on X — CONTACT
- Open with a sample for their brand — PITCH

#### 06 Author’s Instagram clip

- Qualified leads — 900+
- Likes in under 24 hours — 30K
- Views in 24 hours — 200K+
